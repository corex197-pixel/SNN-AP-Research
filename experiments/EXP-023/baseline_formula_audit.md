Official `utils/utils.py::calc_penalty` computes both consumer spatial/flatten K and local spike-derived BN penalties. `penalize` applies weight and neuron updates, then `clear_penalty` resets buffers. The new entry changes only conv penalty buffers after the original calc and before the original penalize; it checks each observed original K per batch and leaves BN penalty buffers untouched.
